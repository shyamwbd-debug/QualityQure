Family Planning & Copper T

Low-cost Copper T insertion and removals, contraceptive methods, and confidential counseling.

Confidential Family Planning & Contraception Counselling

Making decisions about family planning and contraception is deeply personal. At Quality Qure Clinic, we offer private, non-judgmental, and highly confidential counseling sessions to help you choose the contraceptive method that best aligns with your lifestyle, health profile, and reproductive plans. Dr. Jayashree Sharma provides expert guidance and clinical procedures for both short-term and long-term birth control options.

"Family planning is about empowerment. We provide detailed information on all available methods, ensuring you can make informed decisions in a safe, supportive, and private clinical space."

Our Family Planning Services Include:

  • Copper T (IUD) Insertion & Removal: Professional, hygienic, and low-cost intrauterine device (IUD) insertion and removal services, providing highly effective long-term reversible birth control.
  • Oral Contraceptive Counselling: Tailored guidance on oral birth control pills, cycle benefits, side effects, and correct usage guidelines.
  • Injectable Contraceptives: Offering counselling and clinical support for periodic contraceptive injections.
  • Barrier & Emergency Methods: Providing critical advice on barrier contraceptive methods and emergency contraception safety.
  • Permanent Sterilisation Referral: Providing counseling for tubal ligation or vasectomy and facilitating arrangements at surgical centers when desired.

A Private, Compassionate Environment

Your comfort and privacy are our top priorities. All family planning consultations are held in a secure, private room with Dr. Jayashree Sharma. The clinic maintains a welcoming environment where you can ask any questions openly and receive professional advice with complete confidentiality.
