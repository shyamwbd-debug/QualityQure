Meet Your Doctor

Dr. Jayashree Sharma

MBBS, DGO, MRCOG (UK)
Gynecologist, Obstetrician & Infertility Specialist

Dr. Jayashree Sharma has over 27 years of clinical experience in Obstetrics, Gynecology, and reproductive medicine. She practices at Quality Qure Ladies Speciality Clinic in West Mambalam and Saroja Hospital, Chennai.

She completed her MBBS from Tanjore Medical College in 1997, followed by her DGO from Trinity College, Dublin, Ireland in 2005, and earned the prestigious MRCOG from the Royal College of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists, London in 2010. She specializes in handling normal deliveries, fertility counselling, and laparoscopic interventions.
