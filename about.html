Gynaecologist & Obstetrician

Dr. Jayashree Sharma

MBBS, DGO (Ireland), MRCOG (London, UK)

Dr. Jayashree Sharma is a highly recognized Gynecologist, Obstetrician, and Infertility Specialist practicing in Chennai. With 27 years of clinical practice, she is known for providing compassionate, evidence-based medical treatments.

Dr. Jayashree completed her medical graduation (MBBS) from Tanjore Medical College in 1997. She went on to acquire international medical qualifications, earning her Diploma in Gynecology and Obstetrics (DGO) from Trinity College, Dublin, Ireland in 2005. Later, she completed her Membership of the Royal College of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists (MRCOG) from the RCOG, London, UK in 2010.

Through her training in leading European and Indian medical institutions, she has developed specializations in high-risk pregnancies, reproductive endocrinology, infertility treatments, keyhole laparoscopic surgeries, and adolescent health counselling.
