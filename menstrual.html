Menstrual Dysfunctions & PCOS Care

Home  »  Services  »  Menstrual Dysfunctions

Personalized Care for Menstrual Health & Hormonal Balance

Menstrual irregularities and hormonal disorders like PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) are increasingly common in modern times due to lifestyle shifts, stress, and dietary changes. At Quality Qure Clinic, we take menstrual health seriously and believe it is a key indicator of overall wellness. Dr. Jayashree Sharma provides clinical diagnosis, dietary support, and tailored medical therapies to help restore cycle balance and relieve discomfort.

"Many menstrual issues can be resolved or significantly improved through early diagnosis, lifestyle changes, and proper medical counselling. We guide you at every step with patience and attention."

Our Menstrual & Gynecological Services Include:

  • PCOS & PCOD Management: Comprehensive treatment plans combining metabolic support, dietary changes, and cycle regulation therapies.
  • Irregular & Missed Cycles: Clinical diagnosis of cycle irregularities, hormonal analysis, and targeted corrective therapies.
  • Dysmenorrhea (Painful Periods): Diagnosing underlying causes of severe period cramps (such as pelvic congestion or early endometriosis) and offering relief.
  • Heavy Menstrual Bleeding (Menorrhagia): Evaluating causes like hormonal imbalance, endometrial thickness, or polyps, with medical treatment options.
  • Pre-Menopause & Menopause Support: Guiding women through transition symptoms (hot flashes, mood changes) with supportive counseling and medical care.

Empowering Women's Health Education

We prioritize patient education, explaining how stress, sleep patterns, and diet affect endocrine health. Rather than just prescribing temporary patches, Dr. Jayashree Sharma counsels patients on lifestyle habits that contribute to long-term gynecological health.
